# IP and Distribution Boundary

- Do not commit the Core Rulebook PDF or screenshots.
- Do not commit Netflix stills, logos or copied production art.
- Do not reproduce substantial rulebook prose.
- The software may encode functional game mechanics and concise labels/reference metadata.
- Cold Storage story text, factions, pregens and relationship mystery are original project material.
- Before public distribution, obtain/review the relevant Altered Carbon RPG and franchise licensing permissions and replace any trademarked presentation as required.
